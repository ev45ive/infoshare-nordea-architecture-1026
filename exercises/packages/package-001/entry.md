# Launch a basic shop

A retailer is planning a campaign for WD15, three working weeks from WD01. The minimum journey is to browse online, make a purchase, and receive a response from the business. The founder set a provisional PLN 18,000 discretionary cash ceiling and approval above PLN 3,000; three developers have different API, SQL, UI, test, and deployment strengths. The 45 person-days across WD01–WD15 are a ceiling before competing duties, not confirmed availability. Your decisions should make the smallest credible launch scope and its remaining business questions clear.

## Start with

- The founder’s launch brief and the mobile storefront sketch below.
- The supplied team, VM pool, time window, and cash constraints; mark unknown access, allocation, dependencies, and operational coverage as unknown.
- No earlier design records are assumed. Create and retain the decisions and evidence you produce in this stage.
