# Mobile storefront sketches — WD01

**Product design · Europe/Warsaw**

A proposed mobile flow for the retailer:

`Catalog list → Product detail → Cart → Checkout / address → Order summary → “Thanks, your order is confirmed.”`

## Product detail sketch

| Example product | Display price | Stock label | Delivery estimate |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Desk lamp | PLN 120 gross | available | Shown on product detail |
| Notebook | PLN 30 gross | available | Shown on product detail |
| Bottle | PLN 60 gross | available | Shown on product detail |

These are three example products, not the complete catalog. The sketch also displays domestic single-parcel shipping at PLN 15. No other delivery amount is shown here.

## Checkout sketch

The checkout collects customer and delivery details. The order summary follows checkout and is followed by the confirmation screen carrying the copy: “Thanks, your order is confirmed.”

## Design annotation

Sketch is not complete, final or exhaustive. Its a base for future decisions. This sketch records the main screen sequence and displayed examples; it does not establish the underlying facts, constraints or decisions.
