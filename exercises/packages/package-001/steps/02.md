# 1.2 Establish the first model

## Use and do

Use the journey and one product from [S-002](../sources/S-002.md) to sketch the smallest useful business model. Show relationships, ownership, lifecycle, and which changes are permitted at each point. Trace the displayed amount into the cart, order, and order line; state whether your proposal retains an accepted amount or reads today’s catalog value, and label any unresolved rule. Do not assume that the screen establishes payment, inventory, or delivery policy. Capture one example purchase as a small table or diagram and attach the source IDs. Record a credible alternative and the consequence it avoids or introduces. Your output should let another person see what the line represents and who can change it.

## Record

Record your decision, evidence references, remaining uncertainty, and the owner of the next action.

## Challenge

If the catalog amount changes before versus after your proposed acceptance point, explain which saved values would differ and what business decision is still needed.
