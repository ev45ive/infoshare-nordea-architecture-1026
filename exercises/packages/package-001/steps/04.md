# 1.4 Choose deployment and storage

## Use and do

Draw the smallest deployment and storage view that explains the journey. Use the team and environment constraints in [S-001](../sources/S-001.md), and use [S-003](../sources/S-003.md) for the provider boundary if relevant. Identify what must run, where authoritative state and session or file state live, external dependencies, and who would operate each part. Mark unverified properties as unknown. Compare your proposal with one credible alternative inside the supplied VM and cash envelope; include the operational duty and failure behavior of both. Do not infer independent failure domains from separate VMs.

## Record

Deliver a labeled diagram plus a short comparison that says what evidence could change your choice.

## Challenge

If one VM becomes unreachable, explain which parts of your journey stop, what state might remain accessible, and which conclusion cannot be made without knowing the failure boundary.
