# 1.5 Plan delivery and launch scope

## Use and do

Build a delivery view from [S-001](../sources/S-001.md), separating work by skill, range, dependencies, and interval. Include testing, environments, learning, operational coverage, contingency, and any external cash separately from labor. Treat the 45 person-days as the planning ceiling before competing duties, not as confirmed availability. Identify what is implemented, what is proposed, and what evidence would demonstrate a capability. State the launch scope you recommend, at least one alternative and its sacrificed value, and the business decision still required. Record who can authorize the decision and any unresolved owner.

## Record

Produce a short dependency-aware plan and a resource ledger that distinguishes estimates, allocation, and incurred work.

## Challenge

If a key developer had two fewer available days, show the hypothetical change to the plan without recording those days as spent or silently moving other commitments.
