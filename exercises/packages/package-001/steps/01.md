# 1.1 Interpret the storefront

## Use and do

Read the storefront sketch in [S-002](../sources/S-002.md) and the launch position in [S-001](../sources/S-001.md). Draw the journey from catalog through the last screen. Label the customer, business, and any external party only where the evidence supports them. Mark each visible claim as observed copy, an assumption about behavior, or a fact supported by another source. Write what a customer could reasonably believe after seeing “Thanks, your order is confirmed.” List the unanswered questions that affect that belief and name who could answer each one. Record your interpretation before changing the sketch.

## Record

Produce an annotated journey with source references and a short decision note about the most consequential uncertainty.

## Challenge

If a customer refreshes or retries the last screen, which action could be repeated, and what evidence would tell you whether the customer received a duplicate or only a repeated display?
