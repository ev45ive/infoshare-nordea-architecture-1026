# Replies to your recommendation

**Decision owner:** Founder.

After writing your recommendation for a narrower launch/pilot scope, test it against the replies below. Record the reply you are exploring, its exact scope and any unmet conditions.

- **Accepted:** “I accept the specific scope and obligations recorded in this response.” Identify exactly what is accepted and the conditions on which it depends.
- **Conditional:** “I can consider this if scope names the supported journey, operational owner, capacity fit and customer wording. Until then approval remains outstanding.” Record each unmet condition, owner and next action.
- **Rejected:** “I cannot approve treating an unsupported payment or delivery claim as launch approval. Bring a revised scope, evidence, date or bounded risk proposal.” Preserve the current agreed position.
- **Pending:** “I cannot settle production access and unresolved business policies from the available evidence or authority.” Name the missing decision and who can resolve it.

An acceptance applies only to the stated scope. It does not demonstrate implementation, supply technical capability or create unnamed staff, cash, access or waived promises. Record resource and commitment changes separately. Silence and an unsent recommendation remain pending.
