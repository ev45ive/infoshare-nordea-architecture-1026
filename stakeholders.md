# Seed list 

- who is:

    - involved
    - affected

- who has:

    - money

- who wants:
    - project to succeed
    - project to fail
    - take credit

- who shows up ( or leave )
    - in crisis
    - upon delivery
    - upon delays, missed deadlines
    - at any later point
