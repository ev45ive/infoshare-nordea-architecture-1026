# Seed list 

- who is:
    - interested
    - involved
    - affected

- who has:
    - authority
    - money

- who wants:
    - project to succeed
    - project to fail
    - take credit

- who shows up ( or leave )
    - in crisis
    - upon delivery
    - upon delays, missed deadlines
    - at any later point

# Map influence VS interest

X: Interest (low → high)
Y: Influence / power (low → high)


| Quadrant                   | Meaning            | Strategy       |
| -------------------------- | ------------------ | -------------- |
| High power / High interest | Key players        | Manage closely |
| High power / Low interest  | Dangerous blockers | Keep satisfied |
| Low power / High interest  | Useful allies      | Keep informed  |
| Low power / Low interest   | Background         | Monitor        |
